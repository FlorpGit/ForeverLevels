# Pathwise

**Every quest. Every craft. Every level.**

A free, open-source leveling and profession companion for World of Warcraft:
Forever.

## Leveling, 1 to 60

- Routes for every starting race on both sides, from your first quest to
  level 60, moving on to the next zone by themselves.
- Follows your quest log: steps tick off as you take, complete and hand in
  quests. Your class's quests show; others' don't.
- Accepts and hands in your route's quests when you talk to their NPC (hold
  Shift to skip it; it never picks a reward or pays gold).
- A clean waypoint arrow with the distance in yards, the game's own map pin
  and marker at the same place, and pins on the world map and minimap.
- Dungeons and raids for your level, and party sync to see where your group is.
- Don't want a quest? **Skip** it (or its whole chain) and pick one nearby
  instead; the route picks up again after it.
- **Zones for your level**, and class quest, reputation, mount, pet and
  exploration guides in the Routes panel.

## Professions beside your leveling

- Guides from 1 to 300 for all twelve professions.
- One card under your step offers what is worth doing now: train your next
  rank at a trainer on the way, craft what your bags make, or the next step of
  the profession you follow. Go, Later, or skip it. You choose how far:
  Speed, Balanced or Crafting.
- A **Professions dashboard**: your skill, the best craft now and how many
  your bags make, the skill-up chance and crafts to your target, the
  materials still to get for your rank and where (and what a missing one is
  made from), your next rank, and what to disenchant. Tailoring + Enchanting
  players get tips for both.
- Advice only: Pathwise never crafts, buys, sells or disenchants for you.

## Made to look good

Two looks for every window: Modern (dark and quiet, icy-blue highlights) or
Classic (the game's own dialogs, gold headings, red buttons). A compact mode,
window size and opacity sliders, and a movable arrow.

## Pathwise Gear and Pathwise Bags

In the same download: upgrade advice for your spec and Best in Slot (Level);
one bag window with keep and junk markers and junk selling at a click.

## Feedback

Found a wrong step? Click the flag in the guide window: Pathwise prepares a
report for you to paste into its Discord or an email. Nothing is sent from the
game.

## No commands needed

Everything is in Pathwise's own menus: the minimap button (click for the
guide, right-click for settings), the buttons along the guide window, and the
Settings panel. Set keys for the guide, professions, dungeons and settings in
Game Menu > Key Bindings > Pathwise. (Commands still work: `/pw help`.)
