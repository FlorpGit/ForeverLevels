# Pathwise

**Every quest. Every craft. Every level.**

A free, open-source leveling and profession companion for World of Warcraft:
Forever.

## Leveling, 1 to 60

- Routes for every starting race on both sides, from your first quest to
  level 60, moving on to the next zone by themselves.
- Follows your quest log: steps tick off as you take, complete and hand in
  quests. Your class's quests show; others' don't.
- A clean waypoint arrow with the distance in yards.
- Don't want a quest? **Skip** it (or its whole chain) and pick one nearby
  instead; the route picks up again after it.
- **Zones for your level**, and class quest, reputation, mount, pet and
  exploration guides in the Routes panel.

## Professions beside your leveling

- Guides from 1 to 300 for all twelve professions.
- Follow one beside your route: its next step appears in the guide window
  when it is on your way. You choose: Speed, Balanced or Crafting.
- A **Professions dashboard**: your skill, the best craft now and how many
  your bags make, the materials still to get for your rank and where, and
  what to disenchant. Tailoring + Enchanting players get tips for both.
- Advice only: Pathwise never crafts, buys, sells or disenchants for you.

## Made to look good

A dark, quiet interface with icy-blue highlights, a compact mode, window size
and opacity sliders, and a movable arrow.

## No commands needed

Everything is in Pathwise's own menus: the minimap button (click for the
guide, right-click for settings), the buttons along the guide window, and the
Settings panel. Set keys for the guide, professions, dungeons and settings in
Game Menu > Key Bindings > Pathwise. (Commands still work: `/pw help`.)
